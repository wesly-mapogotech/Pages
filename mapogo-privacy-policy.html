Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Welcome to Mapogo. We respect your privacy and are committed to handling information responsibly when you use the Mapogo mobile application and related services.

In summary: Mapogo helps users explore Batak genealogy, including lineages associated with Di Raja Batak and relationships between Batak clans (marga). We do not sell users' personal information.

1. About Mapogo

Mapogo is a mobile application designed to help users explore and understand Batak genealogy, ancestral lineages, family relationships, and information about Batak clans (marga), including genealogical traditions associated with Di Raja Batak.

This Privacy Policy applies to the Mapogo application and services directly associated with it.

2. Information We Handle

The information processed may depend on the features you use and how the application is configured.

2.1 Genealogical and Cultural Information

Mapogo may display genealogical or cultural information such as:

  • Names of ancestors and historical figures;
  • Family lineages and relationships between individuals;
  • Batak clan names (marga);
  • Historical, cultural, or explanatory information about Batak ancestry.

This information is used to provide the application's genealogy features. Genealogical information may relate to historical figures or living individuals. Users should exercise care when submitting or sharing information about identifiable living people.

2.2 Information You Provide

If the application allows you to contact the developer, submit feedback, or contribute genealogical records, information you choose to provide may be processed to handle your request or support that feature.

2.3 Technical and Device Information

When the application connects to online services, the application or its hosting providers may process technical information needed to deliver and secure the service, such as:

  • IP address and technical request logs;
  • Device type and operating system version;
  • Application version and diagnostic information;
  • Security, performance, or error information.

The exact information processed depends on the application's implementation and the services it uses.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used, where applicable, to:

  • Display and navigate Batak genealogical lineages;
  • Provide information about ancestors, Di Raja Batak, and related marga;
  • Respond to questions, feedback, or support requests;
  • Operate, maintain, and troubleshoot the application;
  • Protect the application against misuse and security threats;
  • Improve reliability and comply with applicable legal obligations.
